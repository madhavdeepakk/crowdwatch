Measured on ShanghaiTech Part B, a public benchmark of street scenes with 124 hand-counted people per image on average, using 106 of its test images. Neither model was trained on it. A box detector misses most of a dense crowd, so a density-map network takes over when a zone gets packed.

From 9 hours of simulated crowds that nothing was tuned on. A level must hold for a few seconds before it is raised, which costs a few seconds and removes the chatter. The early warning is weaker: it announced 29 of the 51 events, about 51 seconds ahead, and 42% of its warnings came to nothing.

What is running

  • Detection: YOLOX on ONNX Runtime, on a CPU.
  • Tracking: a ByteTrack-style tracker written from scratch, with its own Hungarian matching.
  • Packed crowds: a DM-Count density map that takes over from the detector.
  • Early warning: a damped trend, tested against each zone's ordinary coming and going.
  • Serving: FastAPI with a WebSocket dashboard, Prometheus metrics and an MCP server for AI assistants.
  • Checks: 100 automated tests, run on every push.

Full method and caveats are in the evaluation and benchmark write-ups.
